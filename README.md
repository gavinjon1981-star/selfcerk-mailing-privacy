We do not use this permission to market unrelated ORDO brands and do not sell your email address to advertisers.

We retain mailing-list information while you remain subscribed or until the list is discontinued. If you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your information, we will stop marketing and delete it unless we need a minimal suppression record or other information to meet legal obligations.
